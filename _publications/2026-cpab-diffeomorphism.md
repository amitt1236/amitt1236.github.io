abstract: >-
  CPAB transformations are always bi-Lipschitz homeomorphisms but not necessarily diffeomorphisms. This note identifies sufficient regularity conditions that guarantee a CPAB transformation is a diffeomorphism, and shows that they are easy to enforce and hold with probability one for common tessellations.
